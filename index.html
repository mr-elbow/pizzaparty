Environment & Fermentation Engine

Step-by-Step Schedule (Target: Saturday @ 5:00 PM)

Thursday, 8:00 PM (36h Prior)

Step 0: The Starter Refresh

Feed your refrigerated starter using your selected ratio (e.g., 1:5:5 uses 25g starter, 125g bread flour, 125g water) to tame acidity and prep robust yeast counts.

Visual Cue: Watch it, don't time it. Look for dome formation, bubbles throughout, and volume doubling/tripling. If peaking overnight, place in the fridge until Friday morning.

Friday, 8:00 AM

1. The Levain Build (With Buffer)

Combine 38g active fed starter, 77g bread flour, and 77g warm water (~85°F). Cover and ferment at room temp until doubled and bubbly (approx. 6–8 hours).

Visual Cue: Look for a domed top, a frothy subsurface network of bubbles, and a sweet, pleasant aroma.

Friday, 4:00 PM

2. Autolyse, Mix & Stretch-and-Folds

Mix flours and 343g water (~72°F) for a 20-min autolyse. Add 162g levain and 18g salt. Mix on Speed 2, incorporating the 25g bassinage water.

Perform 3 sets of stretch and folds spaced 30 minutes apart (4:30 PM, 5:00 PM, 5:30 PM).

Friday, 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM

3. Short Warm Bulk Rise

Let the dough rest covered at room temperature to initiate yeast activity before chilling.

Visual Cue: Look for a modest 20% to 30% volume increase and slight gas bubble formation. Do not wait for a full bulk rise.

Friday, 7:30 PM

4. Balling & Long Cold Ferment

Divide each batch into 3 portions (~381g each for 14" pies), tightly round into smooth balls, place in a sealed container/tray, and put straight into the fridge.

Flexibility Note: This locks in a 1 to 4-day cold retard window. Bake anytime from Saturday through Tuesday!

Saturday, 2:00 PM (3 Hours Before Bake)

5. Counter Relax & Ooni Preheat

Pull dough balls from the fridge 2–3 hours prior to baking to relax and warm up. Fire up your Ooni oven on high for **30–45 minutes** to reach 750°F–850°F on the stone center.
