Step-by-Step Schedule & Visual Cues

Phase 0: Starter Refresh

The Starter Refresh (Overnight Feed)

Feed your refrigerated starter using your selected ratio to tame acidity and prep robust yeast counts before building your levain.

Visual Cue: Watch it, don't time it. Look for dome formation, bubbles throughout, and volume doubling/tripling.

Phase 1

The Levain Build (Buffered)

Combine active fed starter, bread flour, and warm water (~85°F). Cover and ferment at room temp until doubled and bubbly (approx. 6–8 hours).

Visual Cue: Look for a domed top, a frothy subsurface network of bubbles, and a sweet, pleasant aroma.

Phase 2

Autolyse, Mix & Stretch-and-Folds

Mix flours and initial water (~72°F) for a 20-min autolyse. Add levain and salt. Mix on Speed 2, incorporating remaining water via bassinage.

Perform 3 sets of stretch and folds spaced 30 minutes apart.

Phase 3

Short Warm Bulk Rise

Let the dough rest covered at room temperature to initiate yeast activity before chilling.

Visual Cue: Look for a modest 20% to 30% volume increase and slight gas bubble formation. Do not wait for a full bulk rise.

Phase 4

Balling & Long Cold Ferment

Divide dough into equal portions, tightly round into smooth balls, place in a sealed container/tray, and put straight into the fridge.

Flexibility Note: This locks in a 1 to 4-day cold retard window. Bake anytime from Saturday through Tuesday!

Phase 5

Counter Relax & Ooni Preheat

Pull dough balls from the fridge 2–3 hours prior to baking to relax and warm up. Fire up your Ooni oven on high for **30–45 minutes** to reach 750°F–850°F on the stone center.
